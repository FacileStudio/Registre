## 2. What we process, why, and on what basis

- **Your account**: email address, name, language, picture if you add one. To provide your account and sign you in to the Facile apps (performance of the contract, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **How you sign in**: a password stored only as an argon2id hash, the public keys of your passkeys, the sealed seed of your authenticator app, digests of your recovery codes. To secure your account (contract, and legitimate interest in security, art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Sessions**: when you signed in and which apps you reached. To keep you signed in and to sign you out everywhere (contract).
- **Your acceptance of the terms**: which document, which version, when, and a keyed digest of your IP address. To prove consent (legal obligation and legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
- **Spaces and plans**: the shared spaces you belong to, your role, and the plan of each space. To share spaces across apps (contract).
- **Security records**: IP addresses are used in memory to limit abusive requests and are not stored with your account; technical logs are kept for [LOG RETENTION, proposed 30] days (legitimate interest in security).

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and Registre sets no tracking cookie. The only cookie is the session cookie that keeps you signed in, which is strictly necessary and needs no consent.
